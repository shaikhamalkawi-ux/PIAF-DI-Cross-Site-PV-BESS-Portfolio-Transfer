# Licence correction — V04R2

Date: 2026-09-22

The V04R1 public-package plan applied a blanket CC BY 4.0 label to
SlimPark-derived components. V04R2 preserves CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for those
components, supplies source attribution and modification/warranty notices, and
retains the source's consultation request.

The earlier HOLD analysis overstated the need for separate written permission:
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 grants noncommercial sharing and adaptation rights subject to
its terms. No additional permission, consultation, or waiver is asserted. This
correction preserves the Attribution, NonCommercial, and ShareAlike conditions.

Original software remains MIT; other author-owned contributions remain CC BY
4.0. Mixed-component expressions are scoped in `COMPONENT_LICENSE_MAP.csv` and
are not alternative licences. Third-party raw and row-level datasets remain
excluded.

No numerical data, scientific figure, calculation, R6R8 result, or executable
code is changed by this repository correction. The V04R1 approval is retained
as a historical record and remains applicable to author-owned material; it did
not waive third-party restrictions.

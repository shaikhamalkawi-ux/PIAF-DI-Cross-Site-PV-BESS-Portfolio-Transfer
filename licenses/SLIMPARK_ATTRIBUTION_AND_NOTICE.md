## Modifications

Ghassan Malkawi, Ahmed Elsayed, Mohammed Alhagyan, and Bakeel Hussein produced
the PIAF-DI R6R8 derived materials using the included replay procedure. The
transformations include documented date/admission filtering and aggregation,
calibration/holdout partitioning, native and energy-matched PV cases, candidate
quantiles, battery dispatch, portfolio coverage losses, percentiles, and checks.
The derived outputs are neither original raw measurements nor source-endorsed
validation. No scientific values were changed in the V04R2 licence correction.
